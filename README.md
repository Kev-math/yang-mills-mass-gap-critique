An Investigation of a Claimed Continuum Mass-Gap Proof for SU(2) Yang–Mills

Overview

This repository is dedicated to examining mathematical arguments concerning the continuum mass gap in SU(2) Yang–Mills theory.

The investigation focuses on renormalisation-group recursions, scale conversions, index definitions, and asymptotic estimates used in continuum-limit arguments.

The objective is to determine whether the relevant conclusions follow from the stated assumptions and derivations.

No conclusion about the existence or non-existence of a mass gap is asserted without a supporting mathematical argument.

Research status

The investigation is ongoing. Proposed inconsistencies must be checked against the original manuscripts before being treated as established results.

AI assistance

AI tools have been used to assist with drafting and mathematical analysis. Specific claims and derivations require verification against the original sources.
